Choose Your Experience

2 hours · Introductory

Family Rapids

A relaxed introduction to rafting with gentle river sections and time to enjoy the scenery. Your guide explains basic paddle strokes and helps your group practice working together.

Best for: first-time rafters and family groups. Contact us to confirm age and participation requirements.

Includes: guide, raft, paddle, helmet, and personal flotation device.

6 hours · Challenging

Rapid Rush

Spend a full day on an energetic route with sustained paddling and more demanding rapids. Work closely with your guide and crew, with breaks between the active river sections.

Best for: experienced rafters seeking a challenge. Our team will discuss experience and suitability before booking.

Includes: guide, rafting equipment, route briefing, and planned riverside breaks.
